The Marketplace is a catalog of ready-to-use models and skills published by Apers. Anything you get there is added to your [Library](/library/01-overview), so you can use it in any deal.

## What's in the Marketplace?

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Models" icon="cube">
    Pre-built spreadsheet models you add to a deal and tailor to it with Apers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Skills" icon="bolt">
    Instructions Apers reads before a task, so it handles that kind of work in a specific way.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to Browse

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Marketplace">
    Click **Marketplace** in the left side panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a tab">
    **Models** and **AI Skills** list what's available. On **Models**, narrow the list with the **Asset**, **Stack**, **Strategy**, and **User** filters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search">
    Use the search bar to find something specific in the current tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an item">
    Click any item to open its page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each card shows the item's price. Items that come with the Pro plan are marked **included with Pro**, and items you already have are marked **Owned**.

## The Item Page

An item's page describes what it does, its use cases, and its limitations. A model's page also lists its inputs, outputs, and fixed assumptions. The panel on the right shows the price, the format and version, and the buttons for getting and using the item.

Click **Preview** to look at a model before you get it. Some skills have a **Preview Output** button that shows an example of what the skill produces.

## Models vs. Skills

|                       | Models                                              | Skills                                                    |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **What**              | A spreadsheet model                                 | Instructions for Apers                                    |
| **How you use it**    | Add it to a deal and work on it in chat             | Add it to a deal; Apers reads it when it fits the task    |
| **Best for**          | Starting an analysis from a finished structure      | Getting a kind of work done a specific way                |

Many models come with their own skill, which tells Apers how that model is meant to be used.

<Tip>
  You can also ask Apers in a chat which Marketplace models fit your deal. It can tell you whether each one is free, included with your plan, or paid.
</Tip>
