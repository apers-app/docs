Once you've created a playbook, you can deploy it to evaluate any deal.

## Two Ways to Use Playbooks

|                              | Description                                      |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **From Playbooks tab**       | Select a playbook, then choose a deal to evaluate |
| **From a Deal**              | Open a deal, then run a playbook on it           |

## Option 1: From the Playbooks Tab

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Playbooks">
    Go to the left panel and click **Playbooks**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your playbook">
    Click on the playbook you want to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Evaluate Opportunity">
    Click the **Evaluate Opportunity** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a deal">
    In the **Select a Deal to Evaluate** window, choose the deal you want to evaluate and click **Run Playbook**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Option 2: From a Deal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the deal">
    Go to the deal you want to evaluate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Run Playbook">
    Click the **Run Playbook** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a playbook">
    A pop-up window appears. Choose the playbook you want to use and click **Run Analysis**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Either way, the playbook is attached to the deal and Apers starts a new chat that evaluates the deal against your criteria, ending with a recommendation: **Pass**, **Dive Deeper**, or **Invest**.

## Evaluating Under Different Playbooks

A deal has one playbook attached at a time, and running a different playbook replaces it for everyone on the deal. Each run starts its own evaluation chat, so you can still compare how a deal scores under different strategies:

- You want to see how a deal scores under different strategies
- You have different strategies (e.g., Value-Add vs. Opportunistic)
- Different team members have different investment criteria

Run one playbook, then run the next. The earlier evaluation chats stay in the deal's Chat Sessions.
