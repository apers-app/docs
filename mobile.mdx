Open [ai.apers.app](https://ai.apers.app) on your phone and Apers shows a phone-sized view built for checking in while you're away from your desk. There's nothing to install. Tablets get the full desktop app.

## What You Can Do on Your Phone

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="See what needs you" icon="bell">
    The home screen lists **Needs attention** (chats waiting on you, still running, or just finished) and your **Recent chats**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reply and answer questions" icon="reply">
    Reply in any chat. When Apers has asked you a question, answer it right in the chat.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Start new chats" icon="plus">
    Start a quick chat from the home screen, or a new chat in a deal from a chat's header.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Upload and open files" icon="file">
    Attach files to a message, upload them to a deal, and open a deal's files to read them.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Approving Edits

When Apers is waiting for your approval on an edit, the chat shows a one-line summary of what it wants to do, with **Approve** and **Reject** buttons. The full details of an edit show on a computer.

To skip approvals on your phone, turn on **Let Apers make edits without asking (this phone only)** in the chat. It applies to this phone only and doesn't change your setting on your computer.

<Tip>
  After you send a reply or an approval, you can lock your phone. Apers keeps working, and the chat shows **Apers is working. You can lock your phone.** while it does.
</Tip>

## Getting Alerts

Tap **Get alerts on this phone** on the home screen to get a notification when Apers has a question or finishes work you started from your phone.

<Note>
  On an iPhone, alerts only work from the Home Screen app. In Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, and open Apers from there.
</Note>

If your phone has blocked notifications for Apers, turn them back on in your phone's settings.

## What Stays on a Computer

Your phone covers checking in, replying, and quick questions. Use a computer for:

- Building and editing models in the full spreadsheet view, and the full Data Room
- The **Max** and **Alternate model** toggles (see [Chat Modes](/deals/chat-modes))
- Adding credits

<Note>
  Keep the screen open while a file uploads from your phone. Locking the phone mid-upload can interrupt it.
</Note>
