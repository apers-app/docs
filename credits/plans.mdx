Apers offers four subscription tiers designed for different levels of usage. Each plan includes a monthly credit allocation that is added to your balance every month.

## Plan Tiers

<Columns>
  <Card title="Basic" icon="user">
    Designed for individual users getting started with AI-assisted real estate analysis. Includes a monthly credit allocation suitable for moderate, everyday use.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pro" icon="rocket">
    Built for active professionals who rely on Apers daily. Includes a significantly larger monthly credit allocation for heavier usage across chat and modeling.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Max" icon="bolt">
    For the heaviest users. Includes the largest self-serve monthly credit allocation, the lowest price per credit, and priority support. Billed monthly only.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Enterprise" icon="building">
    Tailored for teams and organizations. Offers custom credit allocations, team management features, and dedicated support.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Info>
  Every plan includes chat, AI Excel Modeling, document processing, Playbooks, Workflows, and the Library. Pro and above add Max Mode (the most capable model, for complex analysis) and Marketplace models included with Pro at no extra cost. Beyond that, the main difference between tiers is the monthly credit allocation.
</Info>

## Choosing the Right Plan

- **Basic** is a good starting point if you are evaluating a handful of deals per month and want to explore what Apers can do.
- **Pro** makes sense once Apers becomes a core part of your workflow and you are running models, processing documents, and chatting frequently.
- **Max** fits if you consistently use far more than Pro's allocation and would otherwise be buying credit refills every month.
- **Enterprise** is the right choice when multiple team members need access and you want centralized billing and administration.

<Tip>
  You can change your plan at any time from **Settings** > **Manage Plan**. Upgrades take effect immediately: you pay a prorated amount for the rest of the current period and receive a prorated share of the new plan's additional credits right away. Downgrades take effect at your next renewal.
</Tip>
