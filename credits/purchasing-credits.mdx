If you run out of credits before your next monthly refill, subscribers can purchase additional credits directly in Apers. Purchased credits are added to your balance immediately.

<Note>
  Credit purchases require an active subscription. If you are on a free account, subscribe to a plan first. See [Plans](/credits/plans).
</Note>

## How to Purchase Credits

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Go to **Settings** and click **Refill Credits**. You can also refill from the prompt that appears in chat when your balance runs low.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a credit package">
    Choose from the available credit packages. Larger packages offer better value per credit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete payment">
    Confirm your purchase. Payment is processed through your account's payment method on file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Credits are added instantly">
    Your new credits appear in your balance right away and are available for immediate use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Purchased Credits vs. Plan Credits

| | Plan Credits | Purchased Credits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Source** | Included with your subscription | Bought separately |
| **Cadence** | Added every month on your plan's refill date | Added once, when you buy them |
| **Expiration** | Never expire | Never expire |

<Info>
  All your credits, plan and purchased, share a single balance and never expire. Purchasing simply tops up that balance.
</Info>

## When to Purchase vs. Upgrade

- **Purchase credits** if you occasionally need a top-up before your next monthly refill.
- **Upgrade your plan** if you consistently run out of credits before your refresh date. See [Plans](/credits/plans) for tier details.

<Warning>
  Credit purchases are non-refundable. Review your current balance before purchasing.
</Warning>
