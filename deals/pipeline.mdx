The Deals page offers three ways to view your deals. Choose the one that fits your workflow.

## View Options

<ResponseField name="Table" type="view">
  Displays deals in a spreadsheet-style table with rows and columns.

  Best for: Users with many deals who need to scan and sort quickly.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Pipeline" type="view">
  Displays deals in a CRM-style board with columns for each stage. This is the default view.

  Best for: Managing your deal pipeline and tracking progress.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Map" type="view">
  Displays deals on a map by their address.

  Best for: Seeing where your deals are located.
</ResponseField>

## Using Pipeline View

Pipeline view organizes deals into stages. A new pipeline starts with these default stages:

| Stage       | Description                          |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **New**     | Deals you just added                 |
| **In Review** | Deals you're actively analyzing    |
| **Pass**    | Deals you decided not to pursue      |
| **Invest**  | Deals you're moving forward with     |

You can change them to match your process: click **Configure Pipeline** to add, rename, reorder, or delete stages and group them into phases.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch to Pipeline">
    Click the **Pipeline** view option on the Deals page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag and drop">
    Move deals between columns as their status changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See your pipeline">
    Get a clear view of where all your deals stand.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Pipeline view makes it easy to see your entire pipeline at once and manage deal flow.
</Info>

To customize phases and stages, set stage goals that run automatically, or use Map view, see [Pipeline Automation](/deals/pipeline-automation).
