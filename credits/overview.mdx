Credits are the currency that powers AI features in Apers. Credits are consumed when you work with Apers in chat, including building financial models and reading your documents there.

## How Credits Work

Your account has a credit balance that decreases as you use AI features. The amount consumed varies depending on the complexity and type of task.

| Action | Credit Usage |
| --- | --- |
| **Chat messages** | Varies by conversation length and complexity |
| **AI Excel Modeling** (in chat) | Varies by model size and operations |
| **Reading documents in chat** | Varies by how much of the document Apers reads |
| **Uploading and processing documents** | Free: text extraction and summaries don't use credits |

<Info>
  Your current credit balance is always visible in the dashboard so you can track usage in real time.
</Info>

## Monthly Credit Refresh

Every month, your account receives a fresh allocation of credits based on your subscription plan (annual plans also refill monthly). This allocation is added to your existing balance, so any credits you didn't use carry over.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Subscribe to a plan">
    Choose a plan that fits your usage level. Each plan includes a monthly credit allocation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use credits throughout the month">
    Credits are deducted as you use AI features across Apers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Credits are added on your billing date">
    On your monthly refill date, your plan's allocation is added to your balance. Unused credits from earlier cycles stay in your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Running Low on Credits

If your balance is getting low before the next refresh, you have two options:

- **Purchase additional credits** on top of your plan allocation. See [Purchasing Credits](/credits/purchasing-credits).
- **Upgrade your plan** for a higher monthly allocation. See [Plans](/credits/plans).

## When You Run Out

- **Subscribers** switch to **Eco Mode** automatically when their balance reaches zero. Eco Mode runs chat on a lighter AI model at no credit cost, so you can keep working until your next refill or until you purchase more credits. Max Mode is unavailable in Eco Mode.
- **Free accounts** can't send new messages once their starting credits are used up. Subscribe to a plan to continue.

<Tip>
  Keep an eye on your usage patterns during the first few weeks to find the plan that best fits your workflow.
</Tip>
