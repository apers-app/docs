Every Marketplace item is free, included with the Pro plan, or paid. In all three cases you get it with the same button, **Get**, on the item's page.

## How to Get an Item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the item">
    From the Marketplace, click the model or skill you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the price">
    The panel on the right shows **Free** or the item's price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Get">
    A free or included item is added to your Library right away. A paid item takes you to checkout first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you aren't signed in, **Get** asks you to sign in first.

## Free, Included, and Paid

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Free items">
    The item's page shows **Free**. Click **Get** and the item is added to your Library. There is no checkout.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Items included with Pro">
    Some paid items are included with the Pro plan. With an active Pro, Max, or Enterprise plan, the item's page shows **Free** and **Included with your Pro plan**, and **Get** adds the item to your Library at no charge.

    On other plans, the page shows the price along with **Included Free with Pro Plan**. You can pay for the item on its own, or click **Pro Plan** to look at upgrading. See [Plans](/credits/plans).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Paid items">
    The item's page shows the price. Click **Get** to go to checkout and pay by card. This is a one-time payment for that item, separate from your plan, and it doesn't use Smart Request Credits.

    After you pay, you return to the item's page. It shows **Processing...** for a moment, then **Owned**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## After You Get an Item

The item's page shows **Owned**, and the item is in your **Library** alongside your other files:

- **Models** appear as spreadsheet files.
- **Skills** appear under **Available Skills**.

In the Marketplace, items you have are marked **Owned**, so you can tell what you already own. If you delete an owned item from your Library, open its Marketplace page and use it again from there: Apers puts it back in your Library at no charge.

<Info>
  Items are tied to your account and land in your own Library. To give one to a colleague, see [Sharing with a colleague](/marketplace/using-purchases#sharing-with-a-colleague).
</Info>

## Getting Items from a Chat

You can ask Apers in a chat to recommend Marketplace items. It can add free items, and items included with your plan, to your Library for you. Paid items are always bought from the item's page.
